# Roadmap

## Done

1. **Project.** Godot 4.7 with the Compatibility renderer, the input actions,
   the touch buttons, the jump sound and the export presets of the 2D game.
   The app has its own name (`com.rangelreale.catgame3d`), so it can stay
   installed next to the 2D game.
2. **The cat in 3D.** Rainbow body, paws, tail, wings and a head with ears. It
   walks, jumps with a somersault, flaps its wings three times in the air,
   swims like a mermaid and flies freely. The camera follows the cat, tilted a
   little.
3. **Level 1.** Lawn with a box, a platform, a step, three little monsters, a
   flag, the sun, clouds and hills.
4. **Levels 2, 3 and 4.** Desert, beach with shallow sea and sea floor.

5. **Levels 5 to 16.** Computer room and angry man, forest with the big rat,
   sky with birds, descent through the clouds, inside the airplane, airplane
   flight, park, castle with the princesses, Brazil, giant monster and
   amusement park.

6. **Meta Quest 3 version.** The level appears as a diorama in the real room.
   There is a preview on the computer (`-- --xr-preview`) and the "Quest"
   export. See [04-virtual-reality.md](04-virtual-reality.md).

7. **Title screen, ending and new sounds** (from the 2D game). The game opens
   with "Cat Game" over levels that play by themselves, in sepia, with
   beep music. After the last level come "You won!" and the credits. Enemies
   go "poof" when they vanish, and the big rat and the giant monster go "bonk"
   on every jump before the last.

8. **Portuguese and English** (from the 2D game). The game opens in the device
   language. On the title screen, the flags of Brazil and the United States,
   the L key and the gamepad back button switch the language, and the game
   remembers the choice. On the headset, B or Y switch the language.
